ChoralRoot 0.17 (in progress)

**Fixed: a restart asked from the computer no longer swallows its own answer.** After a restore, and when the
installer tool asks the boot stub for its test or for update mode, the FM-1 answers "accepted" and then restarts.
In 0.16 about one such restart in four left the USB bus before the computer had read that answer: the FM-1 restarted
all the same, but the tool had to guess from the restart that its request was taken ("no answer was seen"). 0.17
leaves the bus only once the computer has taken the answer: about 150 ms after the request as before, later if the
computer is slow to read, and after half a second whatever the computer does.

**The dancing arp is back on the Chord view.** While an arpeggio or a pattern is playing and the view is Chord, the
screen shows the chord's name, its notes on a line and the sounding note hopping from one to the next, as it did
up to 0.13. It shows only while notes are being stepped: let go of the keys (without LATCH), switch the
performance off, press PANIC or stop the loop that was playing it, and the plain chord view is back. LATCH keeps it
going, and so does a playing loop with no key held. The Keyboard, Notes, Geek Out and Scope views are never replaced,
and Strum, Slop and Harp keep the chord view. It shows with Options > Motion set to Off as well (in the first 0.17
test build it did not, and a unit with Motion Off never showed it).

**The PERF layer shows the arpeggio too.** While an arpeggio or a pattern is playing, the top half of the PERF layer
(hold ARP) shows the chord's name and the hopping note instead of the mode picker; the four knob cells under it
stay. The picker always comes first: for two seconds after the layer opens and after every turn of SELECT you see
the mode's name large, also while LATCH keeps the notes going, and then the animation returns. Turning KNOB 1 to
KNOB 4 does not interrupt it.

**One rule for OCT- and OCT+ in every layer.** OCT- is back, OCT+ is confirm. In the PERF and FX layers OCT+ used to
switch the performance or the effects on and off; in the PLAY menu it did nothing. Those layers have nothing to
confirm, so OCT+ now closes them, like OCT-. On / off is a tap of the layer's own button, with the layer open too
(the footer says `ARP: on/off`, `FX: on/off`). Where there is something to confirm OCT+ still does it: the engine
picker's sound, the loop slots' Save / Load / Delete, the save dialog, Options > Calibrate, a rest in step entry.

**Holding a layer's button again closes the layer.** The way in is the way out: KEY, PERF, FX, BASS, METRO, LOOP,
EDIT (the engine picker keeps its sound) and HOME (the view menu). Holding another layer's button still switches to
that layer.

**The KEY layer: MAJ and MIN set the scale, with LOCK on too.** In the KEY layer (hold SEL) the MAJ and MIN chord
keys now pick the scale at the press. Before, MIN only counted while it was held down together with a root, so with
LOCK on (where a chord key is tapped, not held) a minor key could not be set from the keys. In this layer MAJ and
MIN are no longer chord keys: the chord you have latched with LOCK is untouched and is back when the layer closes.
A root on its own now sets the tonic and leaves the scale alone (it used to switch back to major).

**The KEY layer lights its scale keys.** While the layer is open MAJ and MIN glow at half, the key of the selected
scale is fully lit, and the other chord keys are dark. With Options > LEDs on Stock, which has no half level, only
the selected scale's key is lit. Outside the layer the chord keys' lights mean what they always did.

**The KEY layer's keys can play.** Tap GLO (OPT) in the KEY layer and its keys play as they do outside the layer,
chord keys and LOCK included, so you can hear the key while KNOB 1 to KNOB 4 change the tonic, the scale, the
transposition and Single. Tap GLO again and they pick the key as before (a root = the tonic, MAJ / MIN = the scale).
The top of the layer says `keys pick` or `keys play`, GLO is lit while they play, and the choice is remembered after
switching off (it starts on pick). GLO does not open the Options from this layer.

The settings record is version 13. If you go back to 0.16 with the KEY layer's keys left on play, 0.16 reads the
looper's record mode as Overwrite and the PLAY menu's Keys as Play until you set them again; nothing else changes.

**Fixed: PANIC with LATCH on left the next chord latched.** PANIC switched the LATCH light off but the next chord
you played still stayed on after you let go, until LATCH was tapped twice. PANIC now switches LATCH off for good.
