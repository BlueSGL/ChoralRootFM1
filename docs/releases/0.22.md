## Merged after 0.22-beta4

- **VA: finer modulation amounts** (GitHub issue 13): in the VA's modulation matrix (MOD) the Amount now moves in
  tenths below 6 either way (+0.1, +0.2 .. +5.9), and in whole steps from 6 as before; it reads as a number (`+0.30`,
  `+12.00`) instead of a percentage. A vibrato that 1 % made too wide is now a small number: LFO to PITCH at +0.5 is
  about 9 cents with the LFO at full depth (+1, the old 1 %, about 19). Every VA sound plays exactly as before.
  Going back to 0.22-beta4 or earlier: a VA sound saved with an Amount in tenths plays INIT there (its other sounds
  load as before); 0.21 through the device manager gets the nearest whole amount, with a note.
