## Merged after 0.22-beta3

- **A Filter on everything** (GitHub issue 16): the FX layer's eighth page, Filter, is a low cut (Off, 20 Hz .. 2 kHz)
  and a high cut (200 Hz .. 16 kHz, Off) on the whole sound after the effects, 6 or 12 dB an octave, Off on a new
  unit; turning a cutoff switches it on, KNOB 4 takes it out and back to compare; kept by SAVE held. Going back to
  0.22-beta3 or earlier after using it puts the effects' settings and the chord part's sends back to their defaults
  (the record's FX block has more entries than those firmwares read); a unit that never touched it goes back as
  before.
