ChoralRoot 0.21 (in progress)

- **The chorus no longer pops or crackles while you turn Depth.** Until 0.20 every step of the Depth knob made a
  small click under a held sound. Now the chorus slides to the new setting in about a twentieth of a second. With
  the knobs left alone the chorus sounds exactly as it did in 0.20.
- **Chorus Width, on KNOB 3 of the Chorus page** (hold FX, SELECT to Chorus). The chorus has been stereo since 0.19;
  Width sets how far it spreads: 127, where it starts, is the full spread of 0.19 and 0.20, 0 is one chorus in the
  middle, the same in both ears. It turns as smoothly as Depth and is kept with the other effect settings when you
  hold SAVE.

## Going back to 0.20

0.20 and 0.19 have no Width: their chorus is always the full spread, and they ignore the Width stored by 0.21
(nothing else is lost or reset). If you hold SAVE while on the older version, the stored Width is dropped, and 0.21
starts at 127 again when you come back.
