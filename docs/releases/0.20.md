## Chord names: Options > Chord Names

**Lead-sheet chord names, as an option.** Until now a chord was always named the way the Orchid names it: by the
keys you hold. A + 7 + 9 read `A` `7 9`, A + 9 read `A` `9`, and three or four extensions read `JAZZ` and `WTF`.
That is still the default. The new option **Chord Names** (in the Options, after Motion) has two values:

- **Orchid** (default): as before, and as the ORC-1 shows it. Nothing changes if you leave the option alone.
- **Standard**: the name a lead sheet would give the notes that sound.

| You hold | Orchid | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A + 9 | `A` `9` | `Aadd9` |
| A + 7 + 9 | `A` `7 9` | `A9` |
| A + 7 + 6 | `A` `7 6` | `A13` |
| A + 7 + 6 + 9 | `A` `JAZZ` | `A13` |
| A + M7 + 9 | `A` `M7 9` | `AM9` |
| A + 6 + 9 | `A` `6 9` | `A6/9` |
| Am + 9 | `Am` `9` | `Amadd9` |
| Asus + 7 + 9 | `Asus` `m7 9` | `A9sus4` |
| Adim + 6 | `Adim` `6` | `Adim7` |
| Adim + 7 | `Adim` `7` | `Am7b5` |

Good to know:

- **Only the name changes.** The notes, the voicing, MIDI out and your recorded loops are exactly the same under
  either setting, and you can switch whenever you like; the chord on the screen is renamed at once.
- A 6 with a seventh is called a `13`, but the 6th still sounds where it always did, inside the octave.
- A major chord on its own still reads `AM` in Standard, because `A` alone is a single note.
- Two sevenths at once (7 + M7) have no such name, so those chords keep the Orchid's (`7 M7`, `JAZZ`, `WTF`).
  Nothing else reads `JAZZ` or `WTF` in Standard.
- The setting is in the Options, so it is stored by itself, and Reset Settings puts it back to Orchid.

The manual's [Chord names](../MANUAL.md#chord-names) has the rules; [CHORD-NAMES.md](../CHORD-NAMES.md) lists every
chord with its notes and both names.

## Fixes

- **Long secret-chord names were cut on the views.** `m add4` with two or more extensions lost the end of its small
  text on the Chord, Keyboard and Geek Out views (`add4 JAZZ` showed as `add4 JA`). It is whole now; the looper's
  lines always were.
- **Geek Out**: the longest chord names no longer run off the left edge of the screen.
