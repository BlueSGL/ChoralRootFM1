ChoralRoot 0.20 (in progress)

## Chord names: Options > Chord Names

**Lead-sheet chord names, as an option.** Until now a chord was always named the way the Orchid names it: by the
keys you hold. A + 7 + 9 read `A` `7 9`, A + 9 read `A` `9`, and three or four extensions read `JAZZ` and `WTF`.
That is still the default. The new option **Chord Names** (in the Options, after Motion) has two values:

- **Orchid** (default): as before, and as the ORC-1 shows it. Nothing changes if you leave the option alone.
- **Standard**: the name a lead sheet would give the notes that sound.

| You hold | Orchid | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A + 9 | `A` `9` | `Aadd9` |
| A + 7 + 9 | `A` `7 9` | `A9` |
| A + 7 + 6 | `A` `7 6` | `A13` |
| A + 7 + 6 + 9 | `A` `JAZZ` | `A13` |
| A + M7 + 9 | `A` `M7 9` | `AM9` |
| A + 6 + 9 | `A` `6 9` | `A6/9` |
| Am + 9 | `Am` `9` | `Amadd9` |
| Asus + 7 + 9 | `Asus` `m7 9` | `A9sus4` |
| Adim + 6 | `Adim` `6` | `Adim7` |
| Adim + 7 | `Adim` `7` | `Am7b5` |

Good to know:

- **Only the name changes.** The notes, the voicing, MIDI out and your recorded loops are exactly the same under
  either setting, and you can switch whenever you like; the chord on the screen is renamed at once.
- A 6 with a seventh is called a `13`, but the 6th still sounds where it always did, inside the octave (unless you
  turn on the other new option, below).
- A major chord on its own still reads `AM` in Standard, because `A` alone is a single note.
- Two sevenths at once (7 + M7) have no such name, so those chords keep the Orchid's (`7 M7`, `JAZZ`, `WTF`).
  Nothing else reads `JAZZ` or `WTF` in Standard.
- The setting is in the Options, so it is stored by itself, and Reset Settings puts it back to Orchid.

The manual's [Chord names](../MANUAL.md#chord-names) has the rules; [CHORD-NAMES.md](../CHORD-NAMES.md) lists every
chord with its notes and both names.

## 13th chords: Options > 6th with 7th

**The 6th an octave higher when the chord has a seventh, as an option.** The 6 key adds the note 9 semitones above
the root, which puts it right under a seventh: A + 7 + 6 is A C# E F# G, with the F# and the G a semitone apart.
That is how the Orchid plays it, and it stays the default. The new option **6th with 7th** (in the Options, after
Secret Chords) has two values:

- **In place** (default): as before. Nothing changes if you leave the option alone.
- **Octave up**: when a chord has the 6 and a seventh (the 7 key, M7, or both), the 6th sounds an octave higher, on
  top of the chord: a 13th.

| You hold | In place | Octave up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A + 7 + 6 | A C# E **F#** G | A C# E G **F#** (an octave up) |
| A + M7 + 6 | A C# E **F#** G# | A C# E G# **F#** |
| Am + 7 + 6 | A C E **F#** G | A C E G **F#** |
| A + 7 + 6 + 9 | A C# E **F#** G B | A C# E G B **F#** (above the 9th) |
| A + 6, A + 6 + 9 (no seventh) | A C# E F# (B) | the same |

Good to know:

- It works on every chord type, the diminished and the secret chords too, and only when a seventh is held: `A` `6`
  and `A` `6 9` stay as they are. Single notes and the bass do not change.
- **The names do not change.** Orchid naming reads `A` `7 6` either way; Standard reads `A13`, which with Octave up
  is a 13th in fact.
- KNOB 1 voices the raised note as the top note it is: one step down brings it back inside the chord.
- The arp, the strum and the patterns play the notes where they sound, so the 13th is the highest step.
- **A loop follows the option.** A loop holds chords, not notes, so the same loop plays In place or Octave up,
  whichever is set while it plays; nothing in the loop is changed.
- The option acts from the next chord on. A chord that is sounding keeps its notes.
- It is stored by itself, like the other Options, and Reset Settings puts it back to In place.
- If you go back to 0.19 with Octave up set, 0.19 does not know the setting and puts Output, USB Rate and Chord Names
  back to their defaults; set them again there. With In place set, nothing happens.

The manual's [Types and extensions](../MANUAL.md#types-and-extensions) has it;
[CHORD-NAMES.md](../CHORD-NAMES.md#the-6th-above-a-seventh-options--6th-with-7th) lists what it does and does not
touch.

## Fixes

- **Long secret-chord names were cut on the views.** `m add4` with two or more extensions lost the end of its small
  text on the Chord, Keyboard and Geek Out views (`add4 JAZZ` showed as `add4 JA`). It is whole now; the looper's
  lines always were.
- **Geek Out**: the longest chord names no longer run off the left edge of the screen.
