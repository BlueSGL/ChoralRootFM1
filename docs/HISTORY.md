# History: ChoralRoot FM-1, what was done and why

The dated record of the work, moved out of `docs/HANDOFF.md` on 2026-10-11 and kept as it was written (the owner's words
included); the current state is in `docs/HANDOFF.md`. Append-only: a new entry goes at the end, under its day's heading,
and an old entry is never rewritten (a later entry says what changed).

The labels (3b .. 3z, 3y2 .. 3y26, 1 .. 7) are the old hand-over's and other documents cite them. An entry stands under
the day its work was done; one with no date in its text stands under the day it entered the hand-over (by `git log`). An
entry begun on one day and extended later (3b, 3d, 3n, 3q, 3r, 3s) is kept whole under its first day. "The user" and
"the owner" are the same person. The 0.18 entries between 3y16 and 3y17, and "the panel pictures", have no label
and are cited by their titles; 3y17 .. 3y26 are 0.19's, in the order they were committed, and keep the titles in
their headings because other documents cite those too.

3m. **USB recording fixed: done 2026-10-10** (docs/USB-AUDIO.md "What went wrong and why"). A 14 s recording held 6.5 s
(macOS closed and reopened ChoralRoot In every ~250 ms while sound played). Cause: bit stuffing stretched the 540-byte
packets past the bus time the endpoint reserved (exactly 540) on a USB 2 hub; the fix `wMaxPacketSize` 640
(`UA_EP_MAXP`). Also: the ring held until the host's first read (`ua.cap_hold`), a fixed 44.1-frame packet pattern with
a rare one-frame nudge (Melodee's fill servo removed), the endpoint armed at every service call and from the audio ISR
(`audio.c ua_isr_service`) under `usb_sie_owner`; diagnostics: SysEx 73 DEBUG and `fm1_install.py --debug` (counters,
the last 16 events), `tools/uac_check.py` (ffmpeg + mido). Verified on the device: 12.7 / 13.2 s of 14, 27.9 s of 30,
one open. Tests: `cr_usbaudio_test.c` (render gap, hold, frames byte for byte, the 44-run bound). Later, if hubs still
trouble it: a 4-channel or stereo alternate setting (360 / 180 bytes).
