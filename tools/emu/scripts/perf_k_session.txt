# tools/emu/perf.sh (k), the global save: a chord held 1.5 s on TINE EP and let go, then KNOB 1 +2 (the voicing: a session change) and SAVE held: the global save, a new unit's first: the settings and the session written (0.18: saving is manual)
# Nothing is captured in the background any more (until 2026-10-11 the session was captured every 200 ms and written
# after the quiet time). The save: one sector erase an object, 45 ms of the audio ISR held each (16 blocks of silence).
wait 300
key F3 down
wait 40
btn D4 down
wait 1500
btn D4 up
key F3 up
wait 3000
dump                          # 1: nothing saved
knob KNOB1 +2
wait 2000
btn SAVE 900
wait 2100
dump                          # 2: saved: the settings and the session once each
quit
