# ChoralRoot: the update gesture, OCT- + OCT+ + HOME held 5 s (0.18; cr_backup.c cr_update_poll). The two OCT keys
# down: PANIC, as ever; HOME joins them: from 2 s the countdown over the whole screen (3, 2, 1: "update mode: let go
# to cancel" with a boot stub, EMU_STUB=3; "USB boot: .." without); at 5 s the unit leaves: the stub's RESCUE request
# (the staged update loader), or the ROM's USB boot when no stub ran. The emulator prints the restart and ends.
wait 300
knob KNOB1 +2                 # something to save on the way out (the voicing)
wait 200
btn OCT- down
btn OCT+ down
wait 100
btn HOME down
wait 2500
shot build/emu/test/cr_update_3.ppm
dump                          # 1: the countdown at 3
wait 1000
shot build/emu/test/cr_update_2.ppm
wait 3000                     # (the unit left at 5 s: nothing below runs)
dump
quit
