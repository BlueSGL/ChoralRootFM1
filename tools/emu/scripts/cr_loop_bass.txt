# bass-side events, the notes on the engine's main / bass streams.
# A. The Full keyboard, Single = Split Bass (the split at F4): F3 (bass side), A4 (chord side), E4 (bass side), half a
#    second each; the loop plays: F3 and E4 on the bass part (part 1), A4 on the chord part.

# E. The Chords layout, Single = Split Bass (the split point F, by note name): MAJ + G4 a chord, D4 a bass-side note:
#    the loop plays G major on the chord part, D on the bass part.
