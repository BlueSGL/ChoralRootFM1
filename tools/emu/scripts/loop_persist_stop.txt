# Loops are saved by hand (0.18; docs/LOOPER.md "Slots and the record", test_persist.sh). A Free take in slot 1 (D,
# Em: 2 events), stopped: nothing in the flash (dump 1), 3 s later still nothing (dump 2: no write at a stop, none
# when idle). SAVE held: written (dump 3: used 001). Then it is recorded over (Overwrite: REC, G, REC: 1 event) and
# stopped; 3 s: the flash still holds the first loop (dump 4: 1 event in RAM, not saved, no write). Quit: a power
# cycle without a save. loop_persist_back.txt on the same file: the OLD loop (2 events) is back.
wait 300
btn REC
wait 300
btn A#3 down
btn D4 down
wait 500
btn D4 up
btn A#3 up
wait 500
btn G#3 down
btn E4 down
wait 500
btn E4 up
btn G#3 up
wait 400
btn REC                       # the take ends: a 1-bar loop, playing
wait 400
btn PLAY                      # stop
wait 300
dump                          # 1: slot 1, 2 events, stopped, used 000, not saved
wait 3000
dump                          # 2: still nothing written
btn SAVE 900                  # the global save
wait 300
shot build/emu/test/loop_persist_stop.ppm
wait 1500
dump                          # 3: used 001, saved
btn REC                       # recorded over (Overwrite)
wait 300
btn A#3 down
btn G4 down
wait 500
btn G4 up
btn A#3 up
wait 1400
btn REC
wait 400
btn PLAY
wait 3500
dump                          # 4: 1 event in RAM, not saved; the flash as at dump 3
quit
